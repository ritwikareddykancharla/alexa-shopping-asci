01 · Problem

Problem

A living-room voice assistant serves adults, teenagers, children, and visiting guests on the same microphone while operating inside a sub-second spoken latency budget.

Ambient Voice & Safety

Overview

ConversaVoice coordinates three production subsystems on AWS Lambda and Amazon Bedrock. First, a calibrated natural language fast-path classifies unambiguous shopping, age-gated, medical emergency, and crisis turns in 0.04 ms median (75.8% traffic bypass) and escalates polysemous gray-zone turns like "buy chef knives" or "order cider" to Amazon Nova Lite (511.0 ms median) before shaping responses for Adult Verified, Teen, Amazon Kids, and Guest profiles. Second, a phonetic ASR resolver recovers homophones like "usb see hub" and "air pods pro tree" in 1.178 ms median, tracks multi-turn slot refinements on Amazon Nova Micro (337.0 ms median), enforces a strict <= 40-word Alexa SSML spoken budget alongside an Echo Show visual card, and requires explicit voice confirmation before checkout. Third, a Voice-ID SQLite WAL partition isolates household histories with 0 cross-profile leak bytes and tracks federated preference updates with a Rényi Differential Privacy accountant.

01 · ASR & VOICE BREVITY

Misheard Brands and Verbose Replies Break Voice Shopping

Speech recognition frequently transcribes "USB-C Hub" as "usb see hub" or "Tide Pods" as "tied pods", while raw language model replies ramble past 120 words instead of speaking a crisp option under 40 words.

02 · HOMONYM OVER-REFUSAL

Keyword Filters Block Cooking, Music, and History

Simple blocklists falsely refuse "buy chef knives", "Play Killer Queen", "kill port 3000", or "sparkling apple cider", yet running a large model on every utterance adds hundreds of milliseconds to routine battery reorders.

03 · KIDS PROFILE SAFETY

Age-Restricted Items Need Profile-Aware Gates

Hard cider, bourbon, and nicotine gum are valid purchases for a verified adult with a voice PIN, but must be blocked 100% of the time when spoken by an Amazon Kids or unverified Guest profile.

04 · SHARED LIVING ROOMS

Shared Speakers Risk Gift Spoilers and Privacy Drift

Without strict per-speaker partitioning and mathematical privacy accounting, Bob or a visiting guest can uncover Alice's surprise anniversary gift or pharmacy order, and telemetry updates can accumulate unbounded privacy loss.

03 · Implementation

Implementation

Four backend modules implement calibrated content moderation, phonetic voice shopping with SSML compilation, multi-profile SQLite privacy with Rényi DP accounting, and CloudFront Lambda routing.

14 / 14 Unit Tests Passing
01 · ASCI FAST-PATH & BEDROCK CASCADE conversa_voice/asci_cascade.py
Routine shopping turns should never wait half a second for a safety model, yet ambiguous words like "knives", "cider", and "kill" need context so benign cooking and music requests are not mistakenly refused.
CalibratedNLUFastPath fits multinomial logistic weights over word unigrams, bigrams, and character 4-grams, scoring turns in 0.04 ms median (0.116 ms p95) and resolving 75.8% of benchmark traffic locally. High-entropy or polysemous turns escalate to us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0 (511.0 ms median) and pass through _action_for_profile(), achieving 100.0% recall on Kids age-restricted items and 0.0% benign false blocks.
02 · VOICE SHOPPING, ASR & SSML COMPILER conversa_voice/voice_shopping.py
Customers shop across multiple short turns, refine size or price limits verbally, and rely on concise spoken answers paired with visual Echo Show product cards before confirming any purchase.
PhoneticASRResolver combines Metaphone-style consonant fingerprints, Levenshtein distance, and homophone normalization in 1.178 ms median (14 / 14 resolved). VoiceShoppingEngine tracks conversational slots across discovery, narrowing, and CONFIRMED_PURCHASE (+ 4-digit adult PIN), drafts grounded copy via us.amazon.nova-micro-v1:0 (337.0 ms median), and wraps valid <= 40-word Alexa SSML via compile_ssml_budgeted().
03 · HOUSEHOLD ISOLATION & RÉNYI DP conversa_voice/household_privacy.py
Shared kitchen and living-room devices hear multiple family members and guests. Gift orders, wellness items, and children's profiles stay isolated per voice, and federated preference learning caps cumulative privacy expenditure.
HouseholdPrivacyManager scopes SQLite WAL queries by (household_id, profile_id), keeps Guest sessions ephemeral, purges profile rows with PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE) in 0.528 ms, clips per-household gradients to L2 <= 1.0, injects SystemRandom().gauss noise, and converts Rényi divergence across 17 α orders to (ε = 3.3223, δ = 1e-5) (82.3% tighter than naive composition).
04 · SERVERLESS API & BEDROCK CLIENT conversa_voice/server.py · bedrock_client.py
A single routing layer powers both local development and the AWS Lambda Function URL behind Amazon CloudFront, keeping idle infrastructure cost at zero.
route_api_request() and lambda_handler() expose /api/health, /api/voice/turn, /api/asci/moderate, /api/privacy/dp-audit, /api/privacy/purge, /api/benchmarks, and /api/reset, calling us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0 for safety adjudication and us.amazon.nova-micro-v1:0 for low-latency spoken shopping turns.
04 · Trade-Offs

Trade-Offs

Every architectural choice balances voice latency, safety precision, household privacy boundaries, and serverless operating cost.

Design Decisions
01 · TWO-STAGE SAFETY CASCADE

0.04 ms Fast-Path vs. Universal LLM Moderation

Sending 100% of voice turns to a foundation model adds ~500 ms before shopping logic can even start. Routing 75.8% of unambiguous turns through a calibrated n-gram classifier in 0.04 ms cuts median safety overhead to near zero while reserving Amazon Nova Lite (511.0 ms) for genuine lexical ambiguity.

02 · STRICT 40-WORD SPOKEN CAP

Spoken Brevity vs. Spec-Sheet Detail

Capping spoken SSML at <= 40 words (~7 to 12 seconds of speech) omits long comparison tables from audio playback. Pairing one top spoken recommendation and one price contrast with a companion Echo Show visual card preserves fast voice turn-taking without losing visual detail.

03 · MANDATORY CHECKOUT CONFIRMATION

Extra Confirmation Turn vs. Accidental Orders

Requiring an explicit "confirm purchase" turn (plus a 4-digit PIN on 21+ items) adds one conversational step compared to instant 1-shot ordering, preventing misheard background speech or children in the room from placing unintended charges.

04 · GAUSSIAN NOISE VS. MODEL UTILITY

Bounded (ε, δ) Privacy Budget vs. Unlimited Telemetry

Clipping household preference gradients to C = 1.0 and injecting Gaussian noise (σ = 1.45) slightly perturbs aggregate vectors (0.9665 cosine similarity to unclipped mean) and halts updates once ε_max is reached, providing a mathematical Rényi DP guarantee against individual household reconstruction.
